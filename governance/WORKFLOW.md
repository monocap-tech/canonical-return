# Research workflow

The `archive/space` snapshots preserve earlier full research notes with their historical assertions. Improvements should enter as new corrections or appendices rather than silently rewriting an earlier snapshot. Use explicit `CANONICAL`, `DERIVED`, `AUDIT-PASS`, `CONDITIONAL`, `OPEN` and `STOP` labels.

Changes that purport to extend a canonical trace/domain theorem, or transfer a result across Suzuki, Connes–Consani, Morishita, or Deninger, require a precise source/target domain, equation, positivity metric, phase and support quantifiers, plus a falsification attempt.

A successful SymPy assertion, Python interval audit, and Lean kernel elaboration are three different grades of evidence. No automated GitHub Actions workflow is added in the initial import; future workflows should be deliberately source-scoped.

Do not change `monocap-tech/weil-lab`, `monocap-tech/provenance`, `monocap-tech/hodge-rh` or their formal acceptance ledgers from this repository. Public publication does not imply mathematical certification.
