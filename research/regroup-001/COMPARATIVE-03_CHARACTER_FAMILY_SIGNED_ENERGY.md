## 1. Normalization and no cancellation
For x>1, ramp is sum_{1<=n<=x} Lambda(n) chi(n)(x/n-1); n=0 term vanishes. Exact Gchi for nonprincipal chi is ramp + x L'/L(1,chi); principal is allowed in CharFamily only at level one and gives G_zeta from COMPARATIVE-01. Gm=sum a_i Re Gchi_i with each a_i>0. Mellin transform of nonprincipal Gchi on Re s>2 is -L'/L(s,chi)/[s(s-1)]+L'/L(1,chi)/(s-1). This follows by integrating each ramp term; the zeta principal formula was recorded in COMPARATIVE-01. At a zero rho in 0<Re rho<1 its residue is m_chi(rho)/[rho(1-rho)]. Since conjugation takes Gchi to G_conjchi, the family Mellin residue is W(rho)/[rho(1-rho)] with W=sum_i (a_i/2)(m_chi_i(rho)+m_conjchi_i(rho)), strictly positive on the union of strip zeros. Coincident zeros cannot cancel, and real zero residues are positive. This explains the aggregation mechanism; not a new Landau proof.

## 2. Symmetrized spectral probe
Restrict here to primitive characters (including zeta). The functional equation combined with conjugation preserves each primitive character's strip divisor under rho->1-conj(rho); ordinary conjugation sends it to the conjugate character divisor. Symmetrized weighted divisor W is invariant under both. Put z=rho-1/2 and define Q_F(f,g)=sum_z W(rho) conj(F_f(-conj z)) F_g(z), on compact smooth tests F_f(z)=int f(u) exp(zu)du. Absolute convergence by standard primitive L-function zero count and rapid decay uniformly in the strip. This is the weighted sum of character/conjugate-character signed forms. It is not a formally attached arithmetic operator in our current zeta project. For the exponential probe of COMPARATIVE-01, define Phi_F(t)=sum W(rho) exp(tz)/[rho(1-rho)], real even by symmetries, absolute convergence locally uniform. Its Laplace transform on Re lambda>1/2 is sum W(rho)/[rho(1-rho)(lambda-z)], so the poles and residues coincide with the shifted Mellin observable in Re lambda>0, by the classical logarithmic derivative identity. Exact time-domain elementary corrections for general characters are not needed for this pole correspondence and not certified here. Imprimitive Euler-factor boundary zeros and conductor/gamma terms require separate treatment; no extension to arbitrary automorphic L-functions claimed.

## 3. New arbitrary smooth-probe defect bound (conditional on strip)
Apply the authenticated Deduction.zeroFree_of_rpow_window at theta=1/2+d, 0<d<1/2, to a fixed finite family satisfying its exact premise. Primitive reflection then implies |Re z|<=d. This consumes external zero exclusion at a fixed exponent, not final GRH; no claim of independent route.
For any fixed compact smooth f supported on [-a,a], put A_z=(F_f(z)+F_f(-conj z))/2, B_z=(F_f(z)-F_f(-conj z))/2. Reflection-invariant W makes the cross terms cancel exactly:
Q_F(f,f)=sum W(rho)(|A_z|^2-|B_z|^2).
Writing z=b+i gamma,
B_z=int f(u) exp(i gamma u) sinh(bu)du
= (1/2) int_{-b}^b int u f(u) exp(su)exp(i gamma u)du ds
(with oriented limits). Define A=e^(da)||u f||_1 and B=e^(da)(||(u f)'||_1+d||u f||_1). For each |b|<=d the trivial integral estimate gives |B_z|<=d A; one integration by parts (compact smooth boundary vanishes) gives |B_z|<=d B/|gamma| for gamma!=0. Thus |B_z|<=d (A+B)*2/(1+|gamma|) (a safe loose constant, splitting |gamma|<=1 or >1).
Let C_F=sum W(rho)/(1+|Im rho|)^2<infty by finite family and standard O(T log T) zero count. Then
Q_F(f,f)>=-4 d^2 (A+B)^2 C_F.
The same proof applies to any individual primitive character in the family, using its own multiplicities and reflection (ordinary conjugation not necessary for diagonal reality). For a fixed finite bank f_j on common support, define K_j=2(A_j+B_j); by triangle and Cauchy, the Gram lower bound for any coefficient vector v is -d^2 C_F (sum_j K_j^2)||v||_2^2. This extends COMPARATIVE-02 from one exponential translation probe to any fixed finite smooth trial bank. For a fixed family and fixed bank, the defect vanishes at quadratic rate in d. If arbitrarily small d window hypotheses hold for this same family, PSD follows on every compact smooth bank. If families vary with d, do not assert a uniform aggregate constant; the individual character bound uses its fixed zero count provided it is included each time. No physical log-carrier operator-norm coercivity, explicit numerical C_F, strict positivity, compact-domain attachment or new native theorem. This is a signed reflection estimate using standard Fourier decay, originality unassessed.

## 4. Interpretation
Positive family aggregation avoids residue cancellation; the signed decomposition separates the average and difference of reflected samples, and a narrow strip suppresses the difference at first order in amplitude and second order in energy. This makes a mathematical connection between the fixed-exponent window criterion and the underlying signed-energy geometry. It still uses Landau's zero-exclusion step, and therefore does not supply a new proof of GRH or a shortcut to RECON's critical neutral gate. Direct sum positivity on independently variable test components is distinct from positivity of the aggregated same-test kernel; do not infer component PSD from arbitrary weighted PSD by algebra alone. Here component bounds follow from the individual strip conclusion.
