## 1. Exact local-to-global arithmetic identity
Write w(sigma)=a/x on thick places, a/x1 elsewhere; x<x1. For prime p, omega_p=(1-chi_K(p))/2, define A_p(y,sigma)=omega_p sum_{e>=1, e odd or p=d} (y/p^e-w(-p^e sigma))_+. Here d is ramified prime, p inert gives omega=1 and odd powers, split gives 0, ramified gives 1/2 and all powers. The localAsk formula uses the source placeOfInt convention. forcedPart F(p)=sum_sigma A_p(w(sigma),sigma), gain G(p)=m(sum_{tau in tops} A_p(a,tau)+kF(p)). For 0<=y<=a, nonzero support requires p^e<x1. SizeEstimates.primeSum_eq_sum_localAsk states exactly Ask(y,sigma)=2 sum_{p<U prime} A_p(y,sigma) log p for U>=ceil(x1). Factor 2 is essential: chi_K(p^e)=chi_K(p)^e and Lambda(p^e)=log p, so 2omega selects odd inert powers with factor 2 and all ramified powers with factor 1. This is a finite regrouping, not a prime asymptotic. At y=a, thick partner gives a(1/p^e-1/x)_+, thin partner gives cutoff x1. ShortAndEnough.remainder_eq organizes the place counts into progression 1 mod N0 and full prime sums, as in COMPARATIVE-04. No distribution hypothesis is inserted here.
