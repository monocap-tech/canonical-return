## Required record
For each source or identifiable dependency record:
- Canonical author(s), title, repository URL or publication identifier, original location and exact commit/tag/version/date where available; accessed date and immutable source path, blob/hash or theorem receipt where applicable.
- Relationship: imported, adapted, derived, inspired, compared, independently reconstructed (with evidence), or superseded; distinguish intellectual attribution from actual logical/compiled dependency. Preserve historical attribution even after source removal from active imports.
- Exact consumed theorem, definition, code, dataset or numerical certificate; local destination, modifications, normalization/transport contract, dependency edges and receiving investigation. Mark historical-only versus active; do not infer adoption from shared terminology.
- License, notice, authorship and redistribution conditions; record evidence and unresolved restrictions. Do not publish restricted material or assert license clearance without verification.
- Proof status, hypotheses, source authentication, verification receipt and reproducibility/custody status. Distinguish original external proof, local adaptation, local proof, computational result and formal proof. Mark unknown or missing facts explicitly; never invent citations or claim exhaustive coverage from filenames alone.

## Independence audit
For any claimed independent Weil/RH proof, trace the actual terminal theorem's transitive logical dependencies to exact sources, including vendored overlays, imported theorem bodies, definitions and assumptions, and distinguish mathematical dependencies from runtime/build dependencies. Explicitly check for imported RH, GRH, equivalent positivity/zero-exclusion conclusions, or hypotheses whose discharge relies on them, including post-announcement Alpöge proof material. Prior use of external infrastructure is not itself circularity; a standard-axiom audit alone is not proof of route independence. Preserve both positive and negative findings with reproducible evidence. Do not claim independence, originality, or publication-ready attribution before review of the relevant dependency closure.
