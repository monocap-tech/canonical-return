## A. Exact finite rank receiver
For normalized multiplicities U and T_p, W=U-T_p. The selector gives T_p(rho)=0 simultaneously on a finite-height right half-strip beta>1/2, thus W(rho)=U(rho)>0 at any original zero there. Critical reflection rho#=1-conj(rho) gives the same W on the reflected zero. Nonzero residue W(rho)/(rho(1-rho)) follows, not PSD of the contrast.
Let v_z be the evaluation row of a common finite bank of tests at z. For a matrix E of unwanted evaluation rows, a vector c with Ec=0 and v_rho c!=0 exists iff rank([E;v_rho])>rank(E). Proof: annihilator of ker E equals row span E. Two pair coordinates are freely assignable on ker E iff rank([E;v_rho;v_rho#])=rank(E)+2. Proof: rank increment equals rank of pair evaluation restricted to ker E, by rank-nullity. Absence of a twist zero does not imply rank increment on a fixed bank. If unwanted rows include all other distinct zeros in the finite region, the pair is isolated within that truncation. Multiplicities are weights, not repeated independent rows.

## B. Exact reflected determinant
For reflected pair rho != rho#, w=W(rho)=W(rho#) real nonzero, the pair form is 2w Re(a conj(b)), J=[[0,w],[w,0]]. For any two-dimensional complex evaluation compression A, det(A* J A)=-w^2 |det A|^2; full rank iff negative determinant. Eigenvalues of J are +/-|w|. This also holds for the original nonnegative multiplicity w=U(rho); the twist is not necessary for this algebra. Do not identify this reflected form with the predecessor's translation-probe residue weights W/(rho(1-rho)). With real test functions, a nonreal conjugate quartet must be included, or complexification justified. Assign conjugation-compatible evaluations 1,-1 on target pair and conjugates, with sign adjusted to w if necessary; this gives a negative real direction. At real off-line zeros the pair suffices.

## C. Finite interpolation is possible, global control is not supplied
Work in centered Laplace coordinates z=rho-1/2. For a finite distinct set of z values choose a smooth compactly supported bump g whose transform is nonzero at all these points (normalized bump shrunk to the origin tends to 1 at each point). Its translations by jh, j=0,...,N-1, have evaluation matrix diag(F_g(z_i)) [exp(h z_i)^j]. Choose real h>0 sufficiently small avoiding the finite-set resonances exp(h z_i)=exp(h z_k). The Vandermonde determinant is nonzero; thus arbitrary finite evaluation values are realizable on this bank. With a real bump and conjugation-closed data, uniqueness yields real coefficients for conjugation-compatible targets. This is for the ordinary smooth compactly supported test class, not a guarantee for the project's particular fixed bank or all additional physical support/metric constraints. No uniform conditioning, support-independent norm or tail estimate follows. Enlarging the bank and cutoff together may make the tail grow.

## D. Robust determinant and independent proof gap
Let G=A*JA+R be a complete actual two-dimensional compression, where R contains every other zero and all appropriate representation corrections. If ||R||_2 < min(|lambda_-|,lambda_+) of A*JA, the signs persist by the Rayleigh quotient on its two eigenvectors (and the determinant remains negative). Equivalently for real symmetric G=[[a,b],[b,d]] with enclosures, a sufficient test is max over four endpoint products for a*d minus min possible b^2 <0. These are conditional finite certificates, not actual tail bounds. Even a certified negative contrast compression would not contradict original positivity: a difference of PSD forms need not be PSD (e.g. I-2I=-I), and critical-line negative W also obstructs contrast PSD under GRH. The original form must be the terminal object or an independently proved domination/positivity relation must be supplied. Alpöge uses a one-sided arithmetic bound and Landau, not positivity of the signed contrast. No transfer of these dependencies is authorized or established.
