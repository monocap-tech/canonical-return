## 3. Signed residue contrast — new explicit Weil interpretation
Source isPolar_primeError establishes the residue at s in 0<Re s<1:
r_p,M(s)= [sum_xi (m_xi(s)-m_twist_xi(s))]/[phi(M) s(1-s)].
Define U(s)=sum_xi m_xi(s)/phi(M), T_p(s)=sum_xi m_twist_xi(s)/phi(M). Each is nonnegative and invariant under conjugation and critical reflection on strip zeros (finite families closed under conjugation; imprimitive extra Euler factors have no zeros in open strip). Both have ordinary zero-count growth, from primitive associates. The difference W=U-T_p is signed and can vanish even when U and T_p are positive. Form the two absolutely convergent signed Weil translation probes Phi_U(t), Phi_T(t) as in COMPARATIVE-01/03, separately with U and T_p, for z=s-1/2. Then
Phi_contrast(t)=Phi_U(t)-Phi_T(t)=sum_s W(s) exp(t(s-1/2))/[s(1-s)].
Its one-sided Laplace transform is initially sum_s W(s)/[s(1-s)(lambda-(s-1/2))] for Re lambda>1/2. Normal convergence off the discrete poles continues it meromorphically to Re lambda>0; residues agree with the source transform of R_M(exp(t)) shifted by s=lambda+1/2. Equivalently the positive-axis arithmetic signal is exp(t/2)*R_M(exp(t)) in this transform normalization: multiply by exp(-lambda t) to recover exp((1-s)t). Equality of principal parts in the open right shifted strip, not a full pointwise equality of time signals; pole-free corrections not derived. Difference of the two corresponding Gram matrices is not automatically PSD even if each is PSD. Positive prime weights do NOT imply positive spectral weights for this contrast.

## 4. What prevents cancellation

This is a localized spectral separation mechanism, not a general positivity assertion about the contrast.
