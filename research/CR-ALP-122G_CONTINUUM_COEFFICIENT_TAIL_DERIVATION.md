## 1. Resolvent representation on the full form domain

For zero-extended f on I, define K_beta by this kernel compressed to I, J_beta=2I/beta-K_beta. Plancherel proves J_beta>=0. By nonnegative monotone convergence the full Gamma quadratic form on D is g(0)||f||^2+sum_m <f,J_beta f>. Mixed forms follow by polarization and Cauchy-Schwarz for the positive J_beta series. No unproved interchange of a conditionally convergent signed sum is necessary.

For sine e_j=sin(v_j t)/sqrt(a), epsilon=(-1)^j, s=-epsilon, solve u''-beta^2 u=-2beta e_j with u'(0)=beta u(0), u'(d)=-beta u(d). Direct differentiation and boundary substitution give K_beta e_j=2beta e_j/(beta^2+v_j^2)+f_beta(v_j) E_beta/sqrt(a), with f_beta(v)=v/(beta^2+v^2), E_beta=exp(-beta t)+s exp(-beta(d-t)). Uniqueness follows from the two homogeneous Robin conditions (or the integrated energy identity). Integration of E_beta against same-parity e_i gives 2(1-epsilon exp(-d beta)) f_beta(v_i)/sqrt(a).
