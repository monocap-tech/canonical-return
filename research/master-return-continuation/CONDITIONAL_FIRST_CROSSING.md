## 2. Aperture-local arithmetic form
Let F be unitary angular Fourier transform with negative sign and factor (2*pi)^(-1/2). L(f)=integral log(1+r^2)|Ff(r)|^2 dr. H_a is the space of L2 functions supported a.e. on [-a,a] with finite L, norm D squared = ||f||_2^2+L(f). Fix any finite A>0. Work in H_A and identify H_a as its closed linear subspace for 0<a<=A. The support projection is closed in L2, so also in H_A; the weighted space is Hilbert. H_a has dense interior smooth core: use IV241's dilation s^(-1/2)f(x/s), uniformly bounded in D for s in [1/2,1] by log(1+(r/s)^2)<=log(1+r^2)+log4, strong convergence by approximation on continuous compact Fourier functions, then mollify with radius <a(1-s). Thus closure in D of union_{0<b<a} H_b equals H_a. H_A embeds compactly in L2: low-pass truncated to [-A,A] is Hilbert-Schmidt and high frequency tails of D-bounded vectors have squared norm <= L(f)/log(1+R^2). Right continuity of supports: weak H_A / strong L2 limits of vectors supported [-a_j,a_j] with a_j->a are supported [-a,a]; on {|x|>a+epsilon} the sequence eventually vanishes; let epsilon decrease to zero.

Write G(r)=gammaBracket(r), G0=G(0). IV241 establishes
G(r)= (1/2)log(1+r^2)+G0+R(r), 0<=R<=4+log2.
For the core f in H_a, k_f(u)=integral f(x)conj(f(x-u))dx. Fubini gives paperFT k_f(r)=2*pi |Ff(-r)|^2; even G gives integral G|Ff|^2 exactly. Correlation support is [-2a,2a]. Let N_A be any integer > exp(2A), b_n=Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) for n>=2. Literal prime term P(f)=sum_{2<=n<N_A} b_n(k_f(log n)+k_f(-log n)), no tail; |P(f)|<=2 sum b_n ||f||_2^2 by Cauchy-Schwarz and unitary translations. Both pole evaluations give Pi(f)=2 Re(M_+(f)conj(M_-(f))), M_±=integral_{-A}^A f(x)e^(±x/2) dx. On H_a these reduce to the same integrals over [-a,a]. Both moments have squared functional norm S_A=e^A-e^(-A); hence |Pi(f)|<=2 S_A||f||_2^2. The sharper negative eigenvalue bound S_A-2A is available but unnecessary. These identities follow from the reopened literal source on smooth cores for every finite aperture, not by extrapolating a numerical radius-two bound. This is the unique continuous Hermitian extension of the original arithmetic RHS on the interior core. Restrictions from H_A to H_a agree exactly because the additional translation terms vanish, pole integrals agree, and Fourier term is unchanged. In particular

Thus Q is weakly lower semicontinuous along weak-H_A, strong-L2 sequences: L is weakly lsc (bounded linear map to weighted Fourier L2), B_A continuous in L2. ## 3. Abstract first-crossing theorem (proved)
Hypotheses: H_a as above (or nested closed subspaces of a common Hilbert form domain with injective compact physical embedding, right support continuity and left form-core density); consistent continuous Hermitian Q; aperture-local estimate Q>=c_A||.||_D^2-C_A||.||_2^2, c_A>0, and weak-H_A/strong-L2 lower semicontinuity (the estimate and continuity already imply this by shifting Q to a nonnegative continuous quadratic and subtracting the compact physical mass). Initial strict positivity: exists a0>0 such that Q(f)>0 for all 0!=f in H_a0. Failure means a finite A>a0 and f in H_A with Q(f)<0. No positivity assumption at larger radii.

Define lambda(a)=inf{Q(f):f in H_a, ||f||_2=1}. Each set nonempty for a>0, lower bounded by -C_A. Minimizing sequence has bounded D norm by Garding and bounded energies. Hilbert weak subsequence, compact embedding strong L2, closed support, and lsc give a minimizer with norm one. Hence strict initial positivity implies lambda(a0)>0, not merely >=0. Nesting gives nonincreasing lambda. Left continuity at a in (a0,A] follows from form-density of interior supported functions: approximate a normalized minimizer in H_a by f_j in H_bj, bj<a, normalize in L2, energies converge by form continuity. Monotonicity implies left limit equals lambda(a). Right continuity at a in [a0,A) follows for a_j decreasing to a: normalized minimizers bounded in H_A by common lower estimate and uniform upper bound lambda(a0); weak subsequence and strong L2 give a unit limit supported in H_a by right support continuity; lsc implies lambda(a)<=lim lambda(a_j), opposite inequality by monotonicity. Thus lambda continuous on [a0,A]. With lambda(A)<0, the first zero a*=inf{a in [a0,A]:lambda(a)<=0} satisfies a0<a*<A, lambda(a*)=0. The infimum of negative apertures can be later than the first zero if there is a zero plateau; do not assume strict crossing or unique zero. Choose a unit minimizer f* in H_a*. Q nonnegative on H_a* and Q(f*)=0. For any g in H_a*, the polynomial Q(f*+t g)>=0 for real t forces Re Q(f*,g)=0; replace g by i g to force Im Q(f*,g)=0. Therefore Q(f*,g)=0 for every g in H_a*, f*!=0. Equivalently one can use the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for a nonnegative Hermitian form. This gives exactly the conditional neutral-mode implication, and no derivative of lambda, spectral branch, eigenvalue simplicity or uniform bound at infinity is required.

Even initial nonnegativity suffices for the weaker conclusion of a neutral mode at or beyond a0: if lambda(a0)=0 attainment already gives it; otherwise strict initial gap follows. To deduce positivity at all finite apertures from nonexistence of neutral modes, start with a nonnegative initial form and exclude neutral modes for every finite a>=a0. Global unrestricted test positivity follows if every target test is covered by finite centered apertures up to exact translation invariance, and Q agrees with its original Weil form there. For compact supported tests the centered exhaustion is automatic; if target includes a larger test class, additional approximation/continuity is necessary.
