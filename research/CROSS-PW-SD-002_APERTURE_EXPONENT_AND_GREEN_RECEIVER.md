## PW3: exact aperture growth of the optimal unsigned sampling bound
Work first on actual compact smooth complex physical tests supported in [-t,t], norm E(f)=integral log(2+|2pi xi|)|Fhat(xi)|² dxi, Fourier convention exp(-2pi i xi x), Mellin M(f,w)=integral exp(wx)f(x)dx. Let B(t) be the supremum of the maximum of the two representative/reflected nonnegative multiplicity-weighted sample sums divided by E(f), over nonzero tests. Use actual strip-zero family with positive multiplicities, representatives and reflection covering all nontrivial zeros. Classical unconditional unit-height zero count O(log(3+|n|)) with multiplicity; this must be attached literally in Lean. Write Delta=sup({0} union {|Re rho-1/2|}), 0<=Delta<=1/2.
Lower: pick any zero rho with displacement delta>0, select smooth f supported in [-a,a] with M(f,rho-1/2) nonzero (choose f=exp(-i Im rho x) times a nonnegative nonzero bump). Translate by sign(Re rho-1/2)*(t-a). Mellin evaluation multiplies by exp((rho-1/2)L) and E is unchanged by Fourier phase. Therefore B(t)>=m_rho |M(f,rho-1/2)|² exp(-2delta a)/E(f) * exp(2delta t). If pole-null needed choose f=(d²/dx²-1/4)h of the above type, so M(f,w)=(w²-1/4)M(h,w), nonzero at nontrivial zeros, and both pole moments vanish under translation. This is not a negative signed Weil witness.
Upper: fix eta>0, eta<1/2. Rectangle sampling on horizontal displacement interval [-Delta-eta,Delta+eta] and each angular ordinate unit cell: iterated one-dimensional Sobolev evaluation (FTC+Cauchy-Schwarz) bounds squared sample by a constant depending on eta,Delta times integral of squared M and its first horizontal, vertical and mixed derivatives. Those are transforms of x^j exp(sx)f(x), j=0,1,2, with i factors of modulus 1. Unit-cell count and log-weight comparability on each ordinate cell convert sum into a constant times the integral in s of the weighted Fourier L² energies of these three inputs (angular to Mathlib Fourier coordinate factor 2pi).
For t>=1 choose fixed smooth step cutoffs chi_t identically one on [-t,t], supported [-t-1,t+1], first two derivatives uniformly bounded. Multiplier m_{j,s,t}=x^j exp(sx)chi_t(x). For |s|<=Delta+eta<=1, j<=2, the L1 norms of m and its second derivative are bounded by C(1+t)^3 exp((Delta+eta)(t+1)). Fourier integration by parts twice bounds |hat m(xi)| by this factor times C min(1,|xi|^-2), hence its sqrt-log weighted L1 norm has the same bound. Weight w(xi)=log(2+2pi|xi|) satisfies w(xi+z)<=w(xi)+w(z)<=2 w(xi)w(z)/log 2, since each >=log 2. Weighted Young convolution yields E(m f)<=C(1+t)^6 exp(2(Delta+eta)t) E(f). Integrating over bounded s interval gives B(t)<=C_eta(1+t)^6 exp(2(Delta+eta)t). Thus the exact asymptotic
 lim_{t->infty} log(1+B(t))/t = 2 Delta.

## PW4: positive Green recovery, boundary obstruction

Fix L0>2a. Green function for -d²/dL²+1/4 on [L0,infty) with Dirichlet boundary and excluded exp(L/2) mode:
 G0(L,s)=exp(-|L-s|/2)-exp(-(L+s-2L0)/2)>=0.
 Exact recovery J(L)=exp(-(L-L0)/2)J(L0)+integral_{L0}^infty G0(L,s)F(s)ds.
