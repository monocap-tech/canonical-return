# Scientific Disclosure Charter

**Identifier:** SD-CHARTER · **Revision:** 0.1 · **Date:** 2026-11-27  
**Standing:** Planning and deliberation record; proposed for owner review. Not a scientific claim admission or a manuscript release.

## 1. Purpose and jurisdiction

Establish a coherent, independently inspectable disclosure of the ongoing AI-assisted research program in signed Weil forms and the Riemann hypothesis (RH), together with its methodology of adaptive investigative governance. The disclosure serves scientific readers, formal-methods specialists, research engineers and institutional research leadership. It must remain useful independently of any outreach outcome or ultimate resolution of RH.

The disclosure initiative is an editorial and evidentiary activity, not an eighth mathematical investigation. It consumes verified results from the seven existing lineages, preserves their jurisdictions and original source custody, and neither directs new mathematics nor admits results on their behalf. The central disclosure hub is `monocap-tech/canonical-return`; `monocap-tech/weil-lab` and the respective research branches remain authoritative for their own mathematics and formal evidence. Cross-repository links are immutable where relied upon as evidence. Do not copy or supersede historical ledgers merely for presentation.

## 2. Research framing and claim boundaries

Working series title: **Adaptive Investigative Governance in AI-Assisted Mathematical Research**. Subtitle: *A longitudinal study of coordinated reasoning, formal verification, and research continuity through an investigation of Weil positivity and the Riemann hypothesis.*

The mathematical investigation is the substantive case study; the governance and engineering manuscripts must not imply that the methodology proves RH. Preserve the literal full signed Weil form, including the prime, Gamma and both pole terms, and track test domains, support quantifiers, pairing conventions and positive metrics. A finite or fixed-collar result must not be described as global. A conditional theorem remains conditional until every relevant premise has been discharged.

Use the existing [claim custody policy](governance/CLAIMS.md) and [research workflow](governance/WORKFLOW.md). The [W02 continuation amendment](https://github.com/monocap-tech/canonical-return/blob/d4e482f2f616112dca11f6d8fd803a3dffb5a80c/research/regroup-001/W02_CONTINUATION_AMENDMENT.md) is a historical source for the governance study, not authority to enlarge research permissions. Distinguish mathematical standing, computational or native verification, custody, review and consumer adoption. RH and global Weil positivity are unresolved. No new theorem standing is established by this charter.

## 3. Document series and deliberation mandate

The [disclosure index](disclosure/DISCLOSURE_INDEX.md) identifies SD-00 through SD-07. Before drafting each manuscript, record the intended audience, thesis or research question, scope exclusions, evidence inventory, competing interpretations, principal claim risks, outline, and review requirements. Deliberate structure before prose. Retain failed approaches and negative results when they change the interpretation; do not turn chronological agent output into a substitute for a coherent argument.

Distinguish swarmflow (distributed reasoning throughput) from adaptive investigative governance (coherent progression under evidence, dependency and jurisdiction constraints). Treat this distinction as a proposed methodological contribution to be evaluated, not as an established empirical superiority claim. Clearly separate implemented practices, proposed infrastructure, and inferences about platform behavior.

## 4. Evidence and verification contract

Every material claim receives a stable ID and a record containing its exact assertion, source version and location, hypotheses and quantifiers, evidence class, dependent claims, verification method and outcome, limitations, review state and permitted scope of use. Preserve historical corrections rather than rewriting provenance.

Evidence classes are orthogonal: imported/canonical result, original analytical derivation, conditional result, exploratory numerical diagnostic, directed arithmetic certificate, native kernel acceptance, verified remote custody, independent reproduction, and open/STOP. A successful workflow does not by itself establish a theorem. A same-method replay is not necessarily independent verification. A hash or temporary artifact alone is not durable custody. An analytical proof is not Lean-certified unless the corresponding declaration and assumptions have passed the relevant native checks.

For active evidence, retain exact source, inputs, executable reproduction materials, dependency and environment descriptions, outputs and status. Verify immutable remote identities and retrieve the required payloads, not merely their inventory. Record missing binaries, unavailable artifacts and other limitations; prohibit dependent promotion of provisional evidence. Never represent reconstructed material as original. State whether reproduction was actually performed, by what implementation, and what it checked.

## 5. Scientific publication and typesetting standard

Every released SD manuscript must have a full, readable Markdown version, editable LaTeX source with bibliography, and a compiled PDF. Markdown and typeset text must carry the same substantive claims, hypotheses, equations, citations and limitations. Maintain one authoritative manuscript body or an audited synchronization procedure; differences in layout are permissible, changes in scientific meaning are not.

Use AMS-style mathematical typography for mathematical manuscripts (numbered definitions, lemmas, propositions, theorems and proofs), and a conventional scientific technical-report style for methodological and engineering manuscripts. Include title, authorized author identity, date/version, abstract, keywords or subject classifications where applicable, numbered sections, cited prior work, limitations, AI-use and contribution disclosure, data/code availability and reproducibility statements. Use real affiliations only when authorized. Do not invent identifiers, peer review, external certification badges or institutional endorsement. Choose venue-specific formatting only after reviewing that venue's current requirements.

Each manuscript directory shall contain `MANUSCRIPT.md`, `manuscript.tex`, `references.bib`, `manuscript.pdf`, `CLAIMS.md`, `EVIDENCE.json`, and `SHA256SUMS` at release. Record the TeX engine, package versions or reproducible build environment and build command. Check references, equation numbering, mathematical glyphs, clipping, accessibility and PDF text extraction. Verify compiled PDF bytes in the repository or immutable release asset with recorded SHA-256. If binary transfer or compilation is unavailable, mark D6 blocked; a source file or generated image is not a delivered PDF.

## 6. Deliberation and release gates

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| D0 Charter | Scope, authority, audience and document policy recorded |
| D1 Evidence freeze | Immutable per-lineage source pins, scoped statuses, claim and evidence inventory; no invented metrics |
| D2 Deliberation | Approved document thesis, outline, competing interpretations and exclusions |
| D3 Draft | Complete manuscript with citations and explicit limitations |
| D4 Adversarial review | Mathematical, originality, methodological and claim-boundary challenges; actual reviewer identity/type and disposition |
| D5 Reproduction | Evidence links, source identities, dependencies, reproducible checks and open failures recorded |
| D6 Typesetting | Synchronized Markdown/TeX/PDF, successful build and visual/text QA; durable binary receipt |
| D7 Author approval | Authorship, AI assistance, affiliations, licensing, privacy and disclosure approval |
| D8 Release | Immutable version, public index links, release checksum manifest and changelog verified |

D0–D8 are editorial gates, not permission to progress mathematics. A failed gate is documented and repaired or scoped out transparently. An internal draft may have repository custody without being approved for publication. Do not imply independent external review where only project self-review or automated checking exists. Do not publish personal conversation material, access credentials, identifying account data or unsupported incident diagnoses. Obtain owner approval before releasing sensitive operational evidence or choosing author identity and license. No automatic external outreach.

## 7. Release and repository strategy

Maintain a prominent top-level [scientific disclosure entry point](SCIENTIFIC_DISCLOSURE.md), an index, a claim register, evidence register, review status and changelog. Preserve historical README text and its existing scope disclaimers. Propose a reciprocal reader-facing link in the mathematical repository only with separate authorization; do not modify its acceptance ledgers.

Use planned releases v0.1 (initial methodological and scientific disclosure), v0.2 (consolidated mathematical snapshot), v0.3 (post-regrouping integration, if achieved), and v1.0 (mature independently reviewable package). These are scope labels, not delivery dates or mathematical forecasts. Version updates are append-only at the release level, with corrections explicitly superseding claims. Do not wait for RH resolution to document established methodology, but never advance the claim boundary to meet a release target.
