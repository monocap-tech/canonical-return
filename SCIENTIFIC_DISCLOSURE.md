# Scientific Disclosure Program

**Adaptive Investigative Governance in AI-Assisted Mathematical Research**

*A longitudinal study of coordinated reasoning, formal verification, and research continuity through an investigation of Weil positivity and the Riemann hypothesis.*

**Status: disclosure planning and deliberation.** This is a reader-facing entry point to the proposed documentation series, not a proof announcement, peer-reviewed publication or claim of global Weil positivity.

The research program concerns original signed Weil forms and the mathematical, computational and formal obstacles to establishing positivity. Its methodological case study concerns coordinating specialized investigations while preserving hypotheses, verification boundaries, failed approaches and durable research evidence.

- [Scientific Disclosure Charter](SCIENTIFIC_DISCLOSURE_CHARTER.md) — editorial jurisdiction, claim discipline, publication standards and D0–D8 release gates.
- [Disclosure Index](disclosure/DISCLOSURE_INDEX.md) — eight planned scientific briefs and manuscripts, audiences and deliberation priorities.
- [Existing Canonical Return claim policy](governance/CLAIMS.md) — historical claim boundaries.
- [Weil Lab](https://github.com/monocap-tech/weil-lab) — separate mathematical and formal-verification repository.

Every released manuscript will be available as complete Markdown and editable LaTeX with a compiled PDF, supported by immutable evidence references. The historical research records remain authoritative within their own scope. No finished manuscripts or compiled PDFs are represented as available at this planning stage.

The immediate editorial objective is a frozen evidence inventory and deliberated outlines. Scientific publication, authorship and licensing remain subject to owner approval. The Riemann hypothesis remains unresolved.
